Dahlia is a mandala maker: it draws glowing mandalas from mathematical curves, light and sacred symbols, for pictures, wallpapers and your home screen.

Build each mandala from up to five layers. Every layer has its own style – spirographs, rose curves, sacred geometry, flow fields, 3D forms and more, 36 in all – with its own symmetry, palette and glow. Roll the dice for new combinations; the history arrows take you back. On the play field the mandala changes as you drag a finger across it.

Place a symbol large in the centre: Om, the Tree of Life, the Flower of Life, a lotus, yin and yang, the Dharma wheel, an unalome, the triple moon or the Sri Yantra. Draw it as a line, an outline, filled or in beads, frame it with a ring, a lotus ring, square gates or an ensō, and give it a more organic, hand-drawn character.

Choose a gradient, a sunburst, grain or one of your own photos as background, and make palettes of your own, also from a picture. Add bloom, stars or a vignette, a quote with a soft glow, or draw by hand in rotated symmetry.

Save your mandalas to Photos as a square, in the shape of your screen or in ratios such as 4:5, 2:3 and 16:9, showing the whole mandala or filling the picture, or share them. Start with 21 example favourites, keep your own, and move them as a file between your iPhone and the Mac version.

Put a mandala on your home screen: the widget “Mandala of the day” shows one of your favourites, in three sizes.

The app is free: every style and palette, all effects, three layers, as many favourites as you like, the widget, and saving and sharing at screen size without a watermark. Pro is an optional one-time purchase, no subscription, and Family Sharing is on: up to five layers, emblems, text and quotes, and moving layers and the emblem. It saves at 2048 px for prints and wallpapers, with the mandala placed by hand and brightness, contrast and saturation fine-tuned, as a turning video, and as an SVG line drawing. Pro also brings wallpapers with home and lock screen as a matching pair, and the widget changes to another of your favourites every morning. Try more layers, emblems and quotes in the editor before you buy.

No account, no ads, no tracking. Your mandalas stay on your iPhone.
