# QUOTES-1 · Candidates for the quote collections

Public-domain candidates for ADR-0019, each checked word for word against a fetched edition (26 October 2026). Mathis picks; picked ones go into `android/core/.../quotes/`. Already in the app: 38, 40 and 42.

Fetched 26 October 2026. Word counts include every word in the quote. "Trim" means the quote is part of a longer sentence or verse, cut at a punctuation or verse boundary. No words were left out inside a quote.

Abbreviations: PG = gutenberg.org, PG-DE = projekt-gutenberg.org, WS = de.wikisource.org.

## Already accepted, now verified

**A.** "Nach Innen geht der geheimnisvolle Weg." 6 words
- Novalis (d. 1801), Blüthenstaub, fragment 16 (1798)
- Fetched: https://projekt-gutenberg.org/authors/novalis/books/aphorismen (chapter 1, "I. Blütenstaub"), modern spelling.
- Zeno (http://www.zeno.org/Literatur/M/Novalis/Fragmentensammlung/Bl%C3%BCthenstaub) uses the original spelling. Its file has a broken character in that word ("geheimni?volle"); the 1798 form is "geheimnißvolle". Pick one spelling: PG-DE modern "geheimnisvolle", or the original "geheimnißvolle".
- Trim: a sentence of its own in the middle of a fragment. Fine.

**B.** "Herr: es ist Zeit. Der Sommer war sehr groß." 9 words
- Rilke (d. 1926), Herbsttag, Das Buch der Bilder (1906 edition on WS, 1920 Insel edition on PG). Same wording in both.
- Fetched: https://de.wikisource.org/wiki/Herbsttag and https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/34521
- Trim: line 1 of the poem.

**C.** Wilhelm's actual wording is **"Wer andre kennt, ist klug, wer sich selber kennt, ist weise."** 10 words. It is not "andere", and it is one sentence with a comma, not two sentences. In the edition the two verse lines are "Wer andre kennt, ist klug, / Wer sich selber kennt, ist weise." (the second line has a capital letter because it starts a verse line).
- Laotse, Tao Te King, ch. 33 "Unterschiede des Wesens", translated by Richard Wilhelm (1911; d. 1930)
- Fetched: http://www.zeno.org/Philosophie/M/Laozi+(Laotse)/Tao+Te+King+-+Das+Buch+des+Alten+vom+Sinn+und+Leben/Der+Sinn/33.+Unterschiede+des+Wesens
- Zeno's text comes from a 1972 Diederichs reprint of Wilhelm's translation. The translation itself is unchanged.
- Trim: the first two lines of the chapter.

### Angelus Silesius (d. 1677), Cherubinischer Wandersmann. No translator.
The edition is the Insel selection "Aus des Angelus Silesius Cherubinischem Wandersmann", with modernised spelling: https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/48210. Each quote is one line of a couplet. In that edition each line has a wide gap in the middle (the caesura); here it is shown as a normal space.

19. "Die Ros ist ohn warum, sie blühet weil sie blühet," 9 words. Trim: the first line of the couplet. The second line is "Sie acht't nicht ihrer selbst, fragt nicht, ob man sie siehet."
20. "Halt an, wo läufst du hin, der Himmel ist in dir:" 11 words. Trim: the first line of the couplet. Line 2 is about seeking God elsewhere.
21. "Räum weg und mache Luft, das Fünklein liegt in dir," 10 words. Trim: the first line of the couplet.
22. "Zeit ist wie Ewigkeit und Ewigkeit wie Zeit," 8 words. Trim: the first line of the couplet. Line 2 is "So du nur selber nicht machst einen Unterscheid."
23. "Ein Ding und nicht ein Ding: ein Stüpfchen und ein Kreis." 11 words. Trim: the second line of a couplet. The first line is "Ich weiß nicht was ich bin, ich bin nit was ich weiß:". It reads well with a mandala, but it relies a little on the line before it.

### Lao Tzu, Tao Te Ching, translated by James Legge (1891; d. 1897)
Source: https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/216
