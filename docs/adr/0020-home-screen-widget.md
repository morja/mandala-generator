# ADR-0020 · Home screen widget "Mandala of the day"

Status: accepted, 26 October 2026 (Mathis, UX-1 14, DM-14). Android first; the iPhone widget is the amendment of 12 November 2026 below.

## Decision

1. One widget, "Mandala of the day". Square or upright sizes (2×2 to 4×4) show the picture alone, rendered in the widget's own shape so the whole mandala shows and its background goes on. Wide sizes (width over height above 1.3, e.g. 4×2) show the picture without its text at the left and the words at the right: the favourite's own quote, otherwise a quote of the day from the chosen quote languages.
2. It shows the daily wallpaper's turn: the same ticked favourites (ADR-0014), chosen on the daily wallpaper page also while the daily wallpaper is off. A tap opens that favourite in the editor.
3. Free for everyone; without Pro it stays on the first of the chosen favourites. With Pro it moves on every morning in the same job as the daily wallpaper, which now also runs while only a widget is placed (it then only moves the turn on).
4. Plain `AppWidgetProvider` and `RemoteViews`, no new package (Glance would be one). RemoteViews cannot use the Compose tokens, so `res/values*/widget.xml` repeats the surface and text colours of `Tokens.kt`; keep them in step.

## Consequences

The widget renders a 512 px picture when it is placed, resized or moved on, off the main thread. The morning job is planned while the daily wallpaper is on or a widget is placed (with Pro) and cancelled when neither is.

## Amendment, 5 November 2026 (Mathis)

On Mathis' phone one of three widgets showed the empty text for days while favourites existed: all widgets were rendered one after another inside the update broadcast, which Android ends after about ten seconds. Changes to points 2, 3 and 4:

- **Settings per widget.** Each placed widget has its own settings, kept per `appWidgetId` and removed with the widget: *Mandala* is the daily turn (default, as in point 2 and 3) or one fixed favourite, free for everyone; a deleted fixed favourite falls back to the daily turn. *Appearance* is "Like the app" (default, the app's own light or dark setting), "Light" or "Dark". The settings page (`WidgetSettingsActivity`, Compose) is registered as `reconfigurable|configuration_optional`: placing needs no settings, a long press offers them, and the menu's Dahlia page lists the widgets under "Widgets".
- **Colours.** `res/values/widget.xml` holds the light and the dark values, and the widget sets them on its views itself; `values-night` only decides the card before the first picture.
- **Tap.** A tap opens the app as it was. Only when the widget shows another mandala than the editor, a message offers it with "Open"; nothing is replaced without that.
- **Rendering in a job.** The receiver only asks for a redraw. A JobScheduler job (expedited from Android 12 on) draws the waiting widgets one by one, each with its own try/catch, updates each as soon as its picture is ready and falls back to the favourite's thumbnail when rendering fails. The empty text shows only when there is no favourite at all.

Consequences, in addition: a widget is drawn again when it is placed, resized or reconfigured, after the morning run, and when what it would show changes (a favourite deleted, the daily choice, the app's theme, Pro, the starter favourites written on the first start). Each widget remembers what it last showed, so only the affected ones are drawn.

## Amendment, 12 November 2026: the iPhone widget (PLAN-4 WP-10, IOS-10)

Mathis decided F7 a (free, the daily turn with Pro, ADR-0016 point 4 as amended) and F8 a (seven days ahead), and approved the App Group (A2) and background app refresh (A10). The Android points stay; on the iPhone:

- **WidgetKit, small, medium and large** (`ios/Dahlia/Widget`). Small and large show the picture alone, edge to edge; medium is Android's wide widget (the picture without its words at the left, the favourite's words or the quote of the day beside it). No lock screen widgets (PLAN-4 section 8).
- **The extension never renders.** It has about 30 MB, so it does not link the Kotlin core. The app renders into its App Group (`group.<bundle id>`: `group.one.tearoom.mandala`, and `.debug` for the debug build) the square picture of the next seven days of the daily turn (always, so a newly placed widget is sharp at once), and for each placed widget its shape: the large picture in the large widget's own shape, the picture without words for a medium widget whose favourite has words, for its fixed favourite or for the days it follows. Sizes come from the widget sizes the extension last saw (`context.displaySize`). Pictures are named after what they show (settings and size), so nothing is drawn twice; every favourite's thumbnail is written too, the fallback while a picture is missing. An index (`Widgets/index.json`, written atomically, last) lists the favourites with names, words and pictures, the seven days with their favourite and quote of the day, the app's light or dark setting and the debug mark. Files the old index named stay one round.
- **The turn is a function of the date.** A day starts at 4:00 local time and is numbered like Android's `LocalDate.toEpochDay`; with Pro the n-th of the ticked favourites (all while none is ticked) on day n through the shared `DailyTurn`, without Pro always the first. The extension hands WidgetKit one entry per prepared day at 4:00 and, after the seventh, stays on the last picture and asks again the next day.
- **Rendering ahead** happens at every start and return to the app and on every change Android redraws for (favourites added, changed or deleted, the daily choice, the theme, Pro, the quote languages, the starter favourites of a first start), plus a background app refresh scheduled from the next 4:00 on while widgets are placed. Timelines are reloaded only when the index changed.
- **Settings per widget** through a configuration App Intent (Edit Widget): *Mandala* (daily turn, or one of the favourites by name) and *Appearance* (like the app, light, dark). A deleted fixed favourite falls back to the daily turn.
- **Tap:** a `dahlia://widget?favourite=<id>` link (`dahlia-debug` for the debug build) opens the app as it was and offers the widget's mandala with Android's message when it differs.
- **The Dahlia page lists the placed widgets** from `WidgetCenter` current configurations (size, and the favourite or "name · Daily turn"); on iOS the rows open nothing, since a widget's settings open only from the home screen.
- **Debug builds mark widget pictures** with the icon's dot, drawn by the extension bottom right; the More page has a debug switch (and `-widgetMark NO`) to turn it off for screenshots.

## Amendment, 12 November 2026: debug mark and widget sheet on Android (QA-9)

- **Local builds mark the widget** (debug, fast, free; the global rule of 5 November 2026): `WidgetMark` shows the launcher icon's dot bottom right, set in `MandalaWidget.draw` after the picture, the one place for every layout. The menu's Widgets page has a debug switch (local builds only) to turn it off for screenshots; on by default. The release build's `widget_mark_build` is false and its mark drawable empty, so it never shows; the layouts carry a hidden view for it (the wide layout is wrapped in a frame for that, with no visible change).
- **The widget sheet** (`bin/widget-sheet`, Cockpit `docs/android-widget-sheet.md`) draws the real views (`MandalaWidget.views`) of both layouts in every measured launcher box, with a fixed favourite, day and own quotes from the local builds' assets (`WidgetSheetActivity`, `src/localBuild`). Findings on a layout the launcher does not show in that box are warnings; what it shows must have no errors. Its pictures carry no mark.
- **Words fit by measuring** (amendment, 12 November 2026, Mathis' rule a of QA-9; QA-11): before the views are made, `WidgetMeasure` lays out the quote and author with `StaticLayout` in the widget's own font and sizes at the current font scale, in the box from the widget's options (budgeted 8 % lower for Nova). The wide layout keeps its words only when the room beside the picture is at least 140 dp (`widget_min_text_column`, padding included), at least two lines fit and no shown word is wider than the column; otherwise the widget shows the square picture. The quote gets as many lines as fit above the author and ends after its last whole word with an ellipsis; the author keeps one line with an ellipsis. In the square picture the widget's call shrinks the text layer's size in steps down to 60 % of the favourite's until quote and author fit inside the picture, and leaves the words out below that; core draws as in the app. The empty widget's text gets smaller (down to 10 dp) until it fits with whole words.
