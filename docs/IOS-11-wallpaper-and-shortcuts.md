# Dahlia – Mandala Maker

Session: iOS WP-11 wallpaper and Shortcuts · 12 November 2026

## IOS-11: Wallpaper page, the favourites that take turns, and the Shortcuts action "Mandala of the day" (PLAN-4 WP-11)

What WP-11 of [PLAN-4](PLAN-4-iphone-app.md) built, following Mathis' decisions F5 a (Photos with a guide, the Pro Shortcuts action, a ready shortcut) and F6 c (no Live Photo now). Android reference: `DailyWallpaper.kt`, the pair sheet and the daily page (ADR-0014, UX-1 13). Screenshots: `docs/qa/shots/ios-wp11-2026-11-12/` in the main checkout (ignored by Git).

### What was built

- **Wallpaper page** (menu, new "Wallpaper" tile beside Favourite and Share; `UI/WallpaperPages.swift`): the current mandala filling this iPhone's screen, lock and home screen side by side as Android's pair sheet, the home screen's brightness slider (Pro, 20–100 %, 60 % default, stored in the save choice as on Android), the note that the lock screen takes the save sheet's finish, "Save both to Photos" (Pro) or "Save to Photos" (free, one picture without finish, ADR-0014 point 1), and a short guide how to set it in iOS. Free users see "Home and lock, as a pair · Pro", which opens the Pro sheet.

- **Saving** (`EditorModel+Save.swift`, `saveWallpaper`): the menu closes, the progress shows above the editor, the picture is drawn at the screen's own pixels (1179 × 2556 on the iPhone 15) with Fill's zoom (Android's `setNext`), the pair is finished from one drawing (new Kotlin `IosSave.drawWallpaper` and `DrawnFrame.pair`), the home picture goes to Photos first so the lock picture is the newest. Pro features without Pro go through `mayKeep` first.

- **Daily page** (main menu row "Daily wallpaper" with "Today: name" or the Pro line): the favourites row ("All" or "n of m") opening the picker, the automation guide with "Open Shortcuts", the ready-shortcut button once its iCloud link exists (`ReadyShortcut.link`, nil until Mathis shares it), the widget note; without Pro a Pro row instead of the guide. Debug builds show the action's last run (time, drawn now or ahead, peak memory).

- **Picker** ("Favourites"): a grid with ticks as Android's (UX-1 13), all ticked at first, "Tick all"; taps go through the shared `DailyTurn.toggled`. The choice (`DailyChoiceStore`, observed as `EditorModel.dailyChosen`) is what the widgets' daily turn and the Shortcuts action follow; WP-10's "all favourites" is replaced.

- **One rule for whose turn it is** (`Model/DailyWallpaper.swift`): `DailyWallpaper.favourite` = `DailyTurn.turn` with the ticked favourites and the day number from 4:00 (`WidgetDay`), used by the widgets, the daily page and the action.

- **Shortcuts action** (`Intents/MandalaOfTheDay.swift`): `MandalaOfTheDayIntent` with the parameter "Screen" (lock screen, home screen), runs in the app's process without opening the app, Pro only (the cached purchase, else StoreKit is asked, else "Mandala of the day comes with Dahlia Pro"), returns today's picture as a PNG file. `DahliaShortcuts` (App Shortcut) puts it into the Shortcuts app and Spotlight without setup, with two Siri phrases in ten languages (`Resources/AppShortcuts.xcstrings`, generated by `ios/scripts/gen-strings` from `shortcut_phrase_*`).

- **Rendered ahead** (`WidgetSync`): once the action has run on a phone, every widget sync (launch, return, changes, background refresh) also renders today's and tomorrow's pair into the App Group (`Wallpapers/`, files named by a hash of mandala, finish, home brightness and size), removes older ones (not those written in the last ten minutes), and keeps the background refresh scheduled. Missing pictures are drawn inside the action.

- **Background launch**: when iOS starts the app only for the action, the editor no longer draws its preview or the widget pictures; that starts with the first active scene.

- **Texts**: 22 new iOS texts in ten languages (Sonnet translated, Apple's UI names where known), `pair_home_screen` by Apple's German name "Home-Bildschirm". `gen-strings` now gives a language the iOS English text when the iOS file lacks a key (iOS showed the bare key before, for example the two debug labels of WP-10 in German).

- **Debug hook**: `-wallpaperProbe lock|home` (with `-wallpaperProbeDelay`) removes the pictures and runs the action's code, logging time and peak footprint.

### Findings

1. **The action works in the Shortcuts app** (iOS 27.2 simulator): under the app it shows as "Mandala des Tages" with "Bildschirm: Sperrbildschirm", localized, without any setup; run with the app terminated, iOS launches the app in the background, and the action returns the picture (`de-shortcuts-action-result.png`). The App Intents metadata holds the action, the widget intent and the App Shortcut with both phrases.

2. **Time and memory of the action** (simulator, 12 threads, 1206 × 2622): with the pair rendered ahead it answers in 7–8 ms and the whole process stays at 39 MB. Drawn inside the action: 1.1–1.25 s (2.7 s on a cold first run), peak footprint 320–405 MB, from 40–80 MB at rest. The time is far inside the reported 30 s; the memory is the open risk for the background on the phone (6 threads there, so somewhat less; IOS-7 measured a 1080 video at 280 MB on the iPhone 15). The design makes this rare: the drawing in the action only happens when the app has not run since the mandala, the ticks, the finish or the day changed beyond tomorrow. **Not measured on the iPhone**: the probe launched there did not run, most likely because the phone was locked (the app then starts without a window); the debug line on the daily page shows the figure after Mathis' first automation run.

3. **Storage**: one pair is 9–12 MB of PNG (lock 4.8–6.7 MB, home 4.3–6.0 MB); with today and tomorrow about 22 MB in the App Group, only on phones where the action has run.

4. **iOS 27 moved automations into shortcuts.** The Shortcuts app on iOS 27.2 opens with "Unified automations: automations are now part of your shortcuts; to create one, tap Add"; there is no Automation tab, and a new shortcut starts from "Describe a shortcut" with an "Automation" category in the action list. The guide was first written for iOS 26's Automation tab; it is now written so it fits both ("make a shortcut with Mandala of the day and Set Wallpaper Photo, then let it run every day with a Time of Day automation, Daily, Run Immediately"). The iPhone 15 runs **iOS 26.6.1**, so the device check sees the old layout. "Set Wallpaper Photo" and the automation trigger cannot be tried in the simulator.

5. **Review (Opus) found nine points, all fixed**: the background launch started the editor's preview and widget sync beside the action (now deferred); the screen size was never remembered at launch because no scene existed yet (now on every return); a Pro buyer on a new phone would have been refused until the app ran once (StoreKit is asked now); an action waiting behind the app could draw the same pair twice (checked again on the painter's queue, Kotlin buffers collected after each pair); changing the finish did not render ahead again; a file handed to Shortcuts at 3:59 could be deleted by a sync at 4:00 (ten minutes' grace); free saving drew the home picture for nothing; deleted ticked favourites showed "0 of n" (shown as all, as the rule treats them); a failed preview kept its busy bar.

6. **The painter's queue has utility priority**: the action waits behind at most one widget picture, then draws on the same queue. In the simulator that cost nothing measurable; if the device shows long waits, the action should draw on its own queue with user-initiated priority (memory then doubles while both run).

7. **The free state could not be photographed in the simulator**: `-proOwned NO` still showed Pro, because the simulator holds a cached StoreKit test purchase. The free branches (one picture, Pro row, no guide) are simple and were reviewed in code only.

8. **Two build messages without effect.** `ios/scripts/check` warns "Unable to find extract.actionsdata" in its build-for-testing run, while the app build writes the metadata (checked in the built app). Twice a build printed "error: the following command failed with exit code 0 but produced no further output" (once Debug, once Release); the same build run again, and a Release build from an empty folder, succeeded without it. Noted so nobody chases it; if an archive ever stops on it, the App Intents metadata step is the first suspect.

### Device steps for Mathis (iPhone 15, iOS 26.6.1; the debug build with this package is installed)

Before: open Dahlia Debug once, unlocked, so it remembers the screen and Pro; tick three favourites on Menu → Daily wallpaper → Favourites.

- **Set up**: Shortcuts → Automation tab → New Automation → Time of Day, a time two minutes ahead, Daily, **Run Immediately** → Next → New Blank Automation → add "Mandala of the day" (Dahlia Debug), Screen = Lock screen → add "Set Wallpaper Photo", choose only the lock screen, turn off "Show Preview" and "Crop to Subject" → add "Mandala of the day" again with Home screen → a second "Set Wallpaper Photo" for the home screen only, preview off. If the names differ on the phone, please note the real ones; the guide's texts follow them.

- **Unlocked**: wait for the time with the phone unlocked on the home screen. Write down: did it change without any question or banner to tap? Lock screen and home screen both? Did a notification "Running your automation" appear (that is fine, a confirmation is not)?

- **Locked**: set the time two minutes ahead again, lock the phone, wait. Then wake it: is the new lock screen there? Unlock: the home screen too?

- **Lock and home separately**: delete the home steps for a day; only the lock screen should change. Then the other way round.

- **Depth effect**: on the lock screen, long-press → Customize → Lock Screen: is the depth effect offered for the mandala, and does the clock sit behind part of the mandala or not? With text on the mandala (a favourite with a quote), is the quote covered by the clock?

- **Time and memory**: Menu → Daily wallpaper shows "Debug build, last run of the Shortcuts action: … s, drawn now / rendered ahead, peak … MB". Please send the line after the first run (rendered ahead expected), and once more after this: change the finish in the save sheet, leave the app at once, and run the automation's shortcut (drawn now expected; that is the worst case for time and memory).

- **Saving by hand**: Menu → Wallpaper → "Save both to Photos"; then follow the guide on the page in Photos (Share → Use as Wallpaper → Add, then Customize Home Screen → Photos → the home picture). Do the words on the page match what iOS shows?

### The ready shortcut (Mathis builds it once)

- On the iPhone with the **release** app from TestFlight (the debug app's action has another app name and would not work for others): Shortcuts → + → name it "Dahlia wallpaper" → add "Mandala of the day" (Screen: Lock screen) → "Set Wallpaper Photo" for the lock screen, preview off → "Mandala of the day" (Screen: Home screen) → "Set Wallpaper Photo" for the home screen, preview off. Run it once by hand.

- Share → Copy iCloud Link, and send the link to the master chat. An agent puts it into `ReadyShortcut.link` in `ios/Dahlia/Sources/UI/WallpaperPages.swift`; the page then shows "Add the ready shortcut" with the note that it already holds steps 1 to 3. The automation (step 4) stays the user's own step, iOS does not share automations.

- When Apple renames actions, the shared shortcut keeps working; only rebuild it if the action's parameters change.

### Live Photo (F6 c, not built)

What the device check would need: a Live Photo is a HEIC still plus a short MOV paired by an asset identifier, written with `PHAssetCreationRequest` (`.photo` and `.pairedVideo`); the turning frames exist already (`VideoFrames`). On the iPhone 15: save one by hand from a test build, set it as lock screen wallpaper in Photos, and check whether iOS 26 still plays it when the phone wakes (iOS 17 dropped and partly brought back Live Photo wallpapers), how long it moves, and whether "Set Wallpaper Photo" in Shortcuts keeps the motion (most likely not). Only if it plays on wake is it worth a Pro option in the save sheet's Video tab.

### Selbst entschieden

- Das Bild hat die echten Pixel dieses Bildschirms (1179 × 2556), nicht Androids 2048 px auf der kurzen Seite: scharf genug, weniger Speicher im Hintergrund.

- Ohne Pro speichert die Seite ein Bild ohne Finish (ADR-0014 Punkt 1: bildschirmfüllend ist frei), mit Pro beide Bilder mit Finish und dunklerem Home-Bildschirm.

- Vorausgerendert wird erst, wenn die Aktion einmal gelaufen ist (Merker in der App Group): Wer Kurzbefehle nie nutzt, bekommt keine 22 MB und keine täglichen Renderläufe.

- Heute und morgen werden vorausgerendert, nicht sieben Tage wie bei den Widgets: Ein Paar ist rund 11 MB groß.

- Der Tageswechsel der Aktion ist 4:00 wie bei den Widgets; eine Automation vor 4 Uhr bekommt noch das Bild des Vortags.

- Die Wallpaper-Seite ist eine Kachel neben Favorit und Teilen, die Tagesseite eine eigene Zeile im Hauptmenü, wie auf Android.

- Die Kurzbefehle-Anleitung ist so formuliert, dass sie für iOS 26 und 27 passt; eine eigene Fassung je Version wäre genauer, aber doppelte Pflege in zehn Sprachen.

- Kein Satz-Übersicht („parameterSummary“) für die Aktion: Sie bräuchte Text-Schlüssel mit Platzhalter außerhalb des Katalog-Schemas; Shortcuts zeigt den Parameter darunter.

- Die Aktion fragt StoreKit nur, wenn kein Kauf im Cache ist, damit sie morgens schnell bleibt.

- Dateien jünger als zehn Minuten werden beim Aufräumen nie gelöscht.

- Der Simulator war beim Start schon gebootet und wurde zwischendurch von einer anderen Sitzung (andere App) benutzt; ich habe ihn mitbenutzt und keinen zweiten gestartet.
