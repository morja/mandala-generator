# Dahlia – Mandala Maker

Session: master chat · 12 November 2026

## QA-10: Mathis' notes from the first TestFlight build on the iPhone

Build 1.0 (1), installed through TestFlight on the iPhone 15 on 12 November 2026. Collected as Mathis reports them; fixed later in one cleanup package unless marked urgent.

### Notes

1. **The tour reacts with small delays between steps.** Mathis found this a little irritating; not urgent. Likely cause (IOS-8, finding 3): each step first switches the tab and scrolls the row under the tabs, and only then marks the step settled and draws the rings, so the card and rings appear a moment after the tap. To look at: show the card at once and let only the rings follow, or shorten the settling time; compare with Android's tour on the same steps.

2. **Restarting the tour** works from Menu › Dahlia › Tour; Mathis first looked under "Guide", which opens the website guide. Noted as a hint on wording or order, no change decided.

3. **"Remove them" under "Buy for 3.99 €" in the Pro sheet is unclear on its own.** It removes the Pro parts from the current mandala (for example a third layer, the emblem, the quote) so it fits the free version; from the save sheet it reads "Save without them". Same text on Android (`editor_pro_remove`, German "Entfernen"). Proposal for both platforms: a label that names what goes, for example "Remove Pro parts" / "Pro-Teile entfernen"; Mathis decides. Mathis' iPhone is set to English, so the English text is correct.

4. **Typing in the Text tab: the keyboard covers part of the controls, the picture above stays large, and there is no clear way out.** Mathis has to tap somewhere else to dismiss the keyboard, and the layout then jumps back. To do: a "Done" button above the keyboard (keyboard toolbar), the edited field scrolled into view above the keyboard, the picture folded to its small size while typing and restored afterwards, dismiss on scroll; compare with how Android's Text tab handles the keyboard (insets, `imePadding`). Resolved on 13 November 2026 (IOS-12): while a field of the Text tab has the keyboard the picture folds to its small size and comes back afterwards, the field scrolls to the top of the tab once the picture has folded, a "Done" button sits above the keyboard (the author field's return key is "Done" too), and scrolling, another tab or leaving the tab put the keyboard away. Checked in the simulator on the iPhone SE and the iPhone 18 Pro Max; to check on the iPhone 15.

5. **After the purchase Mathis probably did not see the app's message "Thanks! Pro is unlocked."** Apple's "You're all set" appeared, the Pro marks went away and Menu › Dahlia › Dahlia Pro shows "Thank you for buying Pro", so the purchase itself works. Likely cause: the message is shown while Apple's purchase sheet or Dahlia's Pro sheet is still closing and disappears behind it or before it is visible. To check: show it only after both sheets have closed, and keep it on screen as long as other messages (QA-8 case 1).

6. **Two starter favourites carry the same quote.** Since the new starter at position 2 (12 November 2026), both it and starter 13 (Constellation Web, Constellation Web, Universe) show Walt Whitman's "I exist as I am, that is enough." Mathis may change one of them later (a new favourite of his, or another public-domain quote from the collection on starter 13); Resolved on 13 November 2026: starter 13 is now Mathis' Kabir quote ("Dance, my heart! dance to-day with joy.") on the same layers, with the colours of "Stand out" (QA-12). Applies to Android and iPhone alike.

7. **Double tap on a slider does not reset it to its default, as it does on Android.** `SliderRow` (`ios/Dahlia/Sources/UI/Parts.swift`) puts a simultaneous double-tap gesture on the whole row, but the system `Slider` takes the touches on its track and knob, so a double tap on the slider itself (where Android users tap) does nothing; only the label or the value may react. To do: make the double tap work on the slider line itself (an own slider drawn from the tokens, or a gesture that wins over the system slider), keep the accessibility action, and check it on the device. Resolved on 13 November 2026 (IOS-12): `SliderRow` now uses the system slider (UISlider) with a double-tap recogniser of its own that lets the slider see every touch, so a double tap on the slider line itself, track or knob, resets the setting; the label and the value still react, and the accessibility action stays. Checked in the simulator: double tap on the track resets, dragging the knob still works, a vertical swipe that starts on a slider scrolls the list and leaves the value alone. To check on the iPhone 15.
