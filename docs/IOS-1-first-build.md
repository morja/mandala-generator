# Dahlia – Mandala Maker

Session: iOS WP-1 first build · 12 November 2026

## IOS-1: First iPhone build (PLAN-4 WP-1)

What WP-1 of [PLAN-4](PLAN-4-iphone-app.md) built, the collector measurement on the iPhone 15, the items of section 7 that WP-1 had to check, and what is deliberately left for later packages. Screenshots (local, ignored by Git): `docs/qa/shots/ios-wp1-2026-11-12/`.

### Findings

1. **The app exists and runs.** `ios/Dahlia` is generated by XcodeGen from `ios/Dahlia/project.yml` (A6); the `.xcodeproj`, the framework in `ios/build/` and `Config/Signing.xcconfig` stay out of Git. Debug is `one.tearoom.mandala.debug` named "Dahlia Debug" with the marked icon, Release `one.tearoom.mandala` with the plain one (A1); iOS 26, iPhone only, portrait (F2, F3). The icon is the Android launcher motif rendered by the cli from `mac/icon-settings.json` at zoom 1.45; `ios/Dahlia/scripts/mark-icon.swift` draws Android's mark (dark ring, amber dot) on the debug copy. Scripts: `scripts/generate`, `run-sim`, `run-device`, `build-core` (also Xcode's pre-build phase; it builds only the release framework).

2. **Facade, engine and the latest-wins preview** as PLAN-4 section 3: `IosBridge` in `android/core/src/iosMain` grew the iPhone calls (preview at 512 px with the layer cache, the quick 160 px preview, the square export with whole-percent progress, settings and favourites JSON, a `RenderTicket` Swift cancels from any thread; a cancelled render returns nil instead of throwing into Swift). The `Engine` actor runs on its own serial queue, never on the main thread or Swift's cooperative pool. `EditorModel` follows Android's `collectLatest` pipeline: every change cancels the running render, a recently shown state comes back at once (30 kept), anything else renders after 150 ms of rest, with the quick picture first when a change is expected to take more than a second.

3. **Editor, Layers tab, favourites, persistence, saving.** The picture on top (at most half the height), a tab row with Layers, and in it the layers (select, hide), Style (Android's short names, sorted as Android), Palette (Android's offered palettes plus a hidden one in use), Shape and Light and colour with Android's ranges and defaults, double tap to reset. The 21 starters are written on the first start and the app opens on the icon's starter (`3F655220…`). The favourites sheet opens a favourite (its thumbnail stands in until the preview is drawn) and adds the current one; the star fills when the mandala is a favourite. Settings and favourites are written atomically to Application Support with Android's file names and formats. Saving draws the square at 1024 px with progress and adds it to Photos. All of this checked in the simulator, including a restart (the opened favourite and the added one were still there).

4. **Rules WP-3 will share are kept minimal and marked.** `IosEditing.kt` (iosMain) copies only what WP-1 needs from Android's `Editing.kt`: the style order, the offered palettes, the slider ranges and which styles read density and ripple, the file name. Its header says it goes when WP-3 moves the rules to `android/editing`. Likewise marked as temporary: `Tokens.swift` (copied by hand from `Tokens.kt` until WP-4 generates it), `Texts.swift` (English texts with their Android keys until WP-4's catalogue), `SaveSheet` (until WP-8).

5. **Collector target on the iPhone 15: not confirmed, keep the default.** SPIKE method on the iPhone 15 (SpikeBench with today's core, release framework, 6 threads), worst case and favourite, preview 512 and export 1024, three alternating rounds of the default (autotuned) collector and a fixed 64 MB target; medians of three runs after a warm-up, peak footprint sampled every 2 ms:

   | Round | Collector | Favourite preview | Favourite 1024 | Worst preview | Worst 1024 | Peak (worst 1024) |
   |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
   | 1 | default | 1.06 s | 4.92 s | 3.47 s | 15.98 s | 333 MB |
   | 1 | 64 MB | 1.35 s | 6.40 s | 4.10 s | 17.84 s | 195 MB |
   | 2 | default | 1.37 s | 6.31 s | 4.02 s | 17.85 s | 333 MB |
   | 2 | 64 MB | 1.46 s | 6.74 s | 4.29 s | 18.90 s | 196 MB |
   | 3 | default | 1.50 s | 6.65 s | 4.24 s | 19.04 s | 332 MB |
   | 3 | 64 MB | 1.45 s | 7.11 s | 4.62 s | 20.44 s | 197 MB |

   The phone warms up from round to round (the default's worst export goes from 16.0 to 19.0 s), so each 64 MB run is best compared with the default run just before it: 64 MB is 6–7 % slower in the exports and 3–8 % slower in the previews (round 3's favourite preview is level), never faster. The simulator's 16–23 % gain (SPIKE-4 finding 7) does not hold on the device. What 64 MB does give is about 40 % less peak memory (333 → 197 MB for the worst export, 311 → 184 MB for the favourite's). **Recommendation for A5: keep the default collector** (`Engine.collectorTargetMB = 0`), since A5's condition (faster on the device) is not met. If memory ever becomes the problem (the iPhone 11 with 4 GB, the video export), 64 MB is the known lever at about 6 % speed; that would be a new decision.

6. **Editor feel on a heavy favourite: the picture follows half a second after the finger stops.** Measured on the iPhone 15 with the debug app's drag probe (`-dragProbe YES`): it opens the heaviest starter (four layers: Floral, Waves, Moiré, Sphere Grid), then moves Scale like a finger, a step every 50 ms for a second, three times. Opening it: the first preview after 1.3 s (1.1 s render). After each drag the picture stands 0.51–0.54 s after the last step: Android's 150 ms rest, then 0.35–0.38 s of rendering, since only the moved layer is drawn again (the layer cache keeps the other three). While the finger keeps moving the picture stays as it was, as on Android, and nothing outdated is rendered to the end. In the simulator the same drag catches up after about 0.3 s. Whether that feels right in the hand is Mathis' check on the phone.

7. **`gcMutatorsCooperate` is ignored, and removed.** Linking the framework with it prints "w: Mutators cooperation is not yet supported in CMS GC" (Kotlin 2.4.20, checked again today by linking with `-Pkotlin.native.binary.gcMutatorsCooperate=true`); without it the warning is gone. The line is out of `android/core/build.gradle.kts`.

8. **Photos with add-only access works, an album does not.** Saving asks once with the one usage text ("Dahlia adds the pictures you save to your photo library.") and then adds the PNG with Dahlia's file name. Creating an album with only add-only access makes iOS request full library access (`TCCAccessRequest read-write`), which needs a second usage text and a second permission; so no own album, as planned. The app now asks before drawing, so a refusal costs no render, and a refusal offers Settings in the message.

9. **Android unchanged in behaviour.** Only `android/core` changed: the iosMain facade files and the removed iOS link option. `android/scripts/check` (the pre-commit hook) and `android/scripts/check-full` (parity and release build) are green.

10. **Signing: nothing registered.** Automatic signing used the team's existing wildcard development profile ("iOS Team Provisioning Profile: *", application identifier `6735ZK5WKV.*`), as in the spike; no bundle id was registered and nothing was created in App Store Connect. The debug app is installed on the iPhone 15 (fresh install, not yet opened, so Mathis sees the first start). The release id `one.tearoom.mandala` is registered only when a release build is first archived (WP-9 or WP-13, covered by A1).

### Deliberately missing (later packages)

- Scene, Emblem, Text and Draw tabs; adding, duplicating, deleting, moving and randomising layers; blending and positions; own palettes (WP-5, WP-6).

- Text on the picture: the starters with quotes render without their words until the Core Text renderer comes (WP-6); their thumbnails show the words. Background pictures likewise wait for the ImageIO loader (WP-5).

- Back and forward, the last picture standing in at start, the menu, the die, the play field, selecting and deleting favourites, open and save files, the appearance switch, folding the picture (WP-7). Light mode is reachable in debug builds with the launch argument `-lightTheme YES`.

- Formats, framing, sizes, finish, Pro mark, share, video (WP-8); the purchase (WP-9); widgets (WP-10); wallpaper and Shortcuts (WP-11); tour, About, rating, languages (WP-12); the iOS checks and parity test (WP-2).

### Selbst entschieden

- UI questions sent to the master chat (actions in the navigation bar, a fixed tab row, Save opening a small sheet) are built as recommended. Mathis confirmed all three on 12 November 2026 as option a (F12, F13, F14 of PLAN-4 section 6).

- Favourites as a system sheet with medium and large detents, closed after a favourite is opened (as Android); the save sheet at medium height.

- Messages as an own inverted bar at the bottom like Android's snackbar, four seconds, six with an action.

- SF Symbols for the two toolbar icons, the eye and the chevrons, instead of copying Android's icon paths.

- The picture fixed at the smaller of the width minus 32 pt and half the height; on the iPhone SE that leaves about half the screen for the controls until folding comes.

- Fast preview on (Android's default) with no switch yet.

- Text roles in WP-1: four roles on Dynamic Type (title, heading, body, note) in `Tokens.swift`, marked for WP-4's final list under A9.

- The debug mark on the icon kept at Android's size relative to the whole 108 dp layer (about 15 % of the icon), since Android's quarter of the visible icon looked too large on the full-bleed iOS icon.

- Debug-only aids: the launch arguments `-collectorTargetMB <n>` and `-dragProbe YES`, and timing lines starting with "DAHLIA".

- The report kind IOS for the iPhone app's build reports.
